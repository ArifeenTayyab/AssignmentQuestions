Question3:
	Ans: Abstraction-> A is an abstract class. It defines a template for subclasses and hides implementation details.
		 Encapsulation-> Public, private, protected key words for restricting access.
		 Inheritance-> Classes B,and D inherit from A, gaining access to its members.
		 Polymorphism-> same method of two forms with word overridden. Through PrintName() being overridden in subclasses, you can call a.PrintName() and get different behavior depending on the actual object (B, D, etc.).
